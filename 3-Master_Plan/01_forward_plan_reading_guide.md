## B-lite closure — where the name came from

**Origin:** COMPASS label in [`14_PD12_reassessment_memo.md`](14_PD12_reassessment_memo.md) **Question 2** (2026-06-15), after you re-read Alex’s PD12 guidance. Agents needed a word for “strong enough for Alex, weak enough for v1.”

**Not Alex’s term** — he describes priorities; **B-lite** is our shorthand for the **adopted closure standard**:

| Option | Standard | v1? |
|--------|----------|-----|
| **A** | Model reproduces the phenomenon | Too weak |
| **B (full)** | + measurable + prediction + **parametric identifiability across domains** | Too strong — pulls PD12 Priority 1 |
| **B-lite (adopted)** | Phenomenon + minimal mechanism POC + **≥1 model-guided measurement exported** + **≥1 prediction readout** + honest limits + **D10 frozen bundle** | **Yes** — matches Priority 3 without new science |

**Plain English:** B-lite = “show the mechanism can work, export the measurements the model motivates, name the predictions, state the limits, freeze the folder” — **not** “identify every parameter in Army + basketball + tenure.”

**Where it lives operationally:** [`06_Generative_closure_checklist.md`](06_Generative_closure_checklist.md) C1–C8 + D10 bundle (`#08`). **Five greens** (C1–C4 + C7, optionally C8) = B-lite satisfied for basketball generative leg.
