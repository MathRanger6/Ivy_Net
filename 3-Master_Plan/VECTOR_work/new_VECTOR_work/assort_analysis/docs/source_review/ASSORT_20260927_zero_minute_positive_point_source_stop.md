# 2015 rotation audit: positive points with zero recorded minutes

**Status:** Source check completed; Charles accepted the 22-row fallback on September 27. This note records the construction stop that preceded that decision. No peer-average or playing-time result had been produced at the time of the stop.

The first rotation-audit construction found 37 retained 2015 player-game rows, across 23 games and 37 athletes, with positive points and zero recorded minutes in the frozen ESPN-derived box file. The rows contain 157 points; 30 of the affected athletes have at least twenty other recorded minutes on their selected team. Treating those points as part of a points-per-minute numerator while recording no minutes or played appearance can distort the player's rate and rotation measure.

The rows are saved at `3-Master_Plan/VECTOR_work/new_VECTOR_work/assort_analysis/data/rotation_audit_2015/ASSORT_20260927_rotation_audit_v1_zero_minutes_positive_points_stop.csv`. The stopped run's compact count record and game-team totals sit beside it. In one conspicuous example, the frozen East Carolina rows in game `400586813` assign 21 points to four players with zero minutes, and the team's recorded player-minute sum is only 105. The [independent East Carolina–Connecticut box score](https://www.sports-reference.com/cbb/boxscores/2015-02-04-connecticut.html) gives those players 15, 24, 24, and 28 minutes, respectively; it reports 200 total team player-minutes. The saved [Elon–Virginia University of Lynchburg box score](https://www.sports-reference.com/cbb/boxscores/2014-12-02-elon.html) similarly provides positive minutes for eleven Elon players whose frozen rows show zero.

The bounded 23-game source check used `3-Master_Plan/VECTOR_work/new_VECTOR_work/assort_analysis/code/ASSORT_20260927_zero_minutes_recovery.py`. For every game, it checked the box-score date and both final team scores against `datasets/mbb/mbb_df_sched.csv`, selected the affected team's player table, and required an exact normalized player-name and points match before accepting positive minutes. The row-level statuses, source URLs, and source-page hashes are in `3-Master_Plan/VECTOR_work/new_VECTOR_work/assort_analysis/data/source_recovery_2015/ASSORT_20260927_zero_minutes_recovery_overlay.csv`. All 23 source-page snapshots are isolated in that source-recovery folder.

The 16 source zeros may include appearances shorter than one minute or another reporting limitation; the saved sources do not establish a positive numerical minute value. The six unmatched names require a different source to resolve. Neither category may be assigned invented minutes. Charles accepted applying the earlier missing-minute fallback to these 22 zero-minute contradictions: omit their 42 points and zero minutes from the *new audit's* constructed rates, retain their game identifiers for team coverage, and do not count them as positive-minute appearances. The 15 independently recovered rows remain included. The frozen source file and all historical outputs stay unchanged. The working-copy change and its reason are recorded in `3-Master_Plan/VECTOR_work/new_VECTOR_work/assort_analysis/docs/decisions/ASSORT_20260927_rotation_audit_decisions.md`.

Three of those four have at least twenty minutes on the newly selected team. These incidence figures are **preliminary** until the unresolved 22 rows are treated and the full construction is rerun; the selected-player table and final count have not yet been saved.
