# Romania 2001 four county pilot design decisions

**Decision log, begun October 2 and updated October 18, 2026.** This records choices made in conversation before extending the Alba descriptive analysis to Alba, Caraș-Severin, Galați, and Tulcea. A choice recorded here is not evidence of an empirical effect. Source acquisition and reconciliation are described separately in the [four county source audit](../../outputs/romania_2001_four_county_source_pilot/full_gymnasium_reconciliation.md) and [program identity source gate](../../outputs/romania_2001_four_county_source_pilot/program_identity_source_gate.md).

## Decisions agreed so far

1. **Observed gymnasium peer group.** For a focal applicant, use the *other students listed on the same originating gymnasium's 2001 Ministry admission round webpage* to calculate peer examination performance. Keep students who were placed in another county or unassigned in this peer group. We will call this **applicant peer strength**. The pages describe participating applicants, not necessarily every eighth grader at the gymnasium. The national examination measures performance after time in that gymnasium; it must not be labeled innate or pre gymnasium ability. Charles agreed to this definition for the four county pilot.

2. **Focus on plausible competitors.** The full applicant group remains the source population for the peer calculation, but the descriptive displays must also isolate stronger focal applicants, as the data story mosaic's Panels 7 and 8 do. For the first four county display, retain the Alba bands: examination score in the **75th through 90th percentile** as the proposed band of excellence, and the **top 10 percent** separately. Define bands by examination performance, never by eventual placement. Show applicant and originating gymnasium counts behind plotted cells so that one large school cannot masquerade as many independent environments. Charles agreed to these focal bands; this is a descriptive screen, not a causal identification strategy.

3. **Number of selective program tiers.** The **highest admission cutoff tier within each subject** is the primary success definition. The **highest two cumulative cutoff tiers** are a planned comparison, shown alongside the primary result rather than substituted after seeing it. Every program tied at an included cutoff belongs to that tier. A success still requires an actual placement in one of those programs; merely exceeding its cutoff is insufficient. Charles agreed to this order on October 2.

4. **Filled seats requirement.** A program must have filled **all offered places** to qualify for the primary selective-program ranking. A program with vacancies may have a lowest admitted score, but that score does not establish a binding capacity cutoff. Keep the existing notebook switch for a separately labeled comparison that also allows partially filled programs; do not substitute that comparison for the primary result after viewing curves. Charles agreed on October 2.

5. **Subject grouping.** Rank programs separately within the **six original program categories** for the primary result. A separately labeled comparison may rerank within three broader groups: mathematics and natural sciences; social sciences and philology; technology and vocational. Combining groups changes the set of designated successful destinations rather than merely relabeling it, and does not automatically increase the success count. Charles agreed on October 2.

6. **Vocational placements in the denominator.** Keep applicants with confirmed vocational destinations **in the primary outcome denominator**, as Charles originally requested. They also remain in the gymnasium peer calculation. A separately labeled comparison may exclude vocationally placed applicants from the outcome denominator, while retaining them as peers. That comparison changes the population being described; without recorded preferences it does not reveal who actually competed for academic programs. Charles reaffirmed “keep them initially” on October 2. The saved Alba notebook currently has its exclusion switch on, so the four county run must set this control explicitly.

7. **Placement outside the originating county.** The source audit identifies 444 applicants from the four pilot counties with a placement in another county. Keep each in the observed originating-gymnasium peer group, but exclude them from the **primary within-county placement outcome denominator**. Their placement is real; without a verified ranking of its destination program under the chosen county rule, it must not be coded as failure. Report the excluded number for each originating county. This preserves the original Alba rule and was agreed by Charles on October 2.

8. **Unresolved placement record.** The one gymnasium applicant row without a matching saved placement or unassigned report stays in the peer calculation. Its outcome is **unresolved**, so omit it from the primary success-rate denominator for now and retain it on the open source list. Report a simple one-case bound showing how the rate would change if it ultimately proved to be a qualifying success or a nonsuccess; neither case is asserted as fact. This is a temporary analysis rule, not a decision to write off the source. Charles agreed on October 2.

9. **Focal examination score bands.** Define the proposed 75th–90th percentile band and top 10 percent **within each originating county** for the primary four county comparison, because the primary placement ranking is county based. Publish the actual examination score boundaries for each county. Also show a clearly labeled pooled four county band comparison so the conclusion does not rest silently on local percentile boundaries. This rule describes local standing; the examination itself was national and raw scores remain comparable. Charles agreed, with some hesitation, on October 3; retain the comparison and revisit if it materially changes the interpretation.

10. **Peer strength axis.** Plot the mean **original-scale national examination score of the other observed applicants from the same gymnasium** on the horizontal axis. Keep this scale common across the four counties, and print enough information to relate it to any standardized Alba figures. The peer mean excludes the focal applicant; it is not a measure of innate ability or of every eighth grader at the gymnasium. Charles agreed on October 3.

11. **Locally placed applicants with unclear exact program identity.** On October 3, Charles initially agreed to leave such placements out of the primary success-rate denominator pending a source check. That check found 558 cases with more than one possible directory program: 109 in Alba, 271 in Caraș-Severin, none in Galați, and 178 in Tulcea. Under the agreed **six original categories, fully occupied programs, and top-one or cumulative top-two cutoff tiers**, *none* of the possible programs for any of these 558 cases is a designated success. Their exact destination program remains uncertain, but their binary top-program outcome is known to be **nonsuccess** under both designated tier rules. Charles therefore agreed on October 4 to **include all 558 in the primary denominator as nonsuccesses**, retain them in gymnasium peer calculations, and show a separately labeled comparison excluding them. Keep their exact program identities unresolved in the source record. This classification must be rechecked if the program-grouping or full-occupancy rules change; do not carry it automatically to those alternate specifications. Confirmed unassigned applicants also remain in the primary denominator as nonsuccesses.

## Choices still open

12. **Source gate and K/N.** The offline program-identity gate passed its program-code, capacity, and admitted-count checks in all four counties. It resolved the 558 ambiguous binary outcomes for the top-one and top-two rules without asserting an exact program identity. Before constructing curves, report the observed success fraction, qualifying program places, one-case unresolved-outcome bound, and source exclusions separately. Without applicant preferences, the true number competing for each program cannot be observed, so the observed success fraction is not automatically the institutional \(K/N\).

13. **Source exception.** One of 12,945 gymnasium applicant rows has no matching saved placement or unassigned report row. It is on the Galați originating gymnasium webpage with source code 186. It remains unresolved, not unsuccessful; Charles retains the final decision on any exclusion or source conclusion.

These denominators differ. Incoming origins use recovered gymnasium evidence where available and otherwise the printed origin-school county on the matched county applicant record. Missing or contradictory matches remain separately unresolved. Counts describe school locations, not actual household relocation or applicants' motives.
