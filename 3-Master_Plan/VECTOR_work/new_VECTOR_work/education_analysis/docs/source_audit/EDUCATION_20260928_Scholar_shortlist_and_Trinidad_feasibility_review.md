# Scholar shortlist and Trinidad feasibility review

**Date:** 2026-09-28  
**Status:** Bounded document, literature, and replication-package review completed. No confidential student data were obtained, no analytical code was executed, and no substantive model was fitted.

**Later sequencing update:** The subsequent [`Scholar_EDUCATION_20260928_Public_Options_Romania_Audit_v2.md`](../../../Scholar_EDUCATION_20260928_Public_Options_Romania_Audit_v2.md) identified a public Romanian replication archive that may preserve prior transition score, assigned high school/track/cohort, and later Baccalaureate outcomes. Original VECTOR is auditing that schema. The Trinidad findings below remain valid, but the recommendation to proceed immediately with NELS:88 and HS&B:80 is paused until the bounded Romania gate is resolved. This update records the changed evidence rather than silently rewriting the earlier judgment.

## 1. Why we performed this check

The Scholar VECTOR search produced five serious candidates rather than an open-ended catalogue. It evaluated prior ability or performance, assignment, peer-group identification, sorting, congestion or scarcity, selection intensity \(K/N\), later outcomes, longitudinal linkage, and identification opportunity separately.

The shortlist identifies the Trinidad and Tobago centralized secondary-school assignment system as the strongest overall scientific match. This review asked only whether that candidate is usable on the dissertation timetable and whether the posted evidence supports the claimed mechanism components.

## 2. What the Trinidad setting actually contains

The confidential Secondary Entrance Assessment (SEA) records contain:

- a pre-assignment entrance-examination score;
- each student's ranked school choices;
- the school to which the student was assigned;
- the student's SEA cohort; and
- the information required to reconstruct the centralized assignment process and school-specific score cutoffs.

The public replication code preserves common school codes and cohort identifiers. Therefore, if the confidential records were obtained, we could construct school-by-cohort peer groups, describe realized sorting using incoming SEA performance, calculate leave-one-out peer measures, and estimate a sorting index.

Later records add secondary examinations, tertiary qualifications, scholarships, arrests, births, and formal labor-market participation. These outcomes make the setting much richer than a single test-score study.

The published paper uses plausibly exogenous variation generated by the rule-based assignment system. This is a real identification opportunity: students near assignment thresholds can be compared even though their assigned schools differ. Rich covariates alone would not provide that protection.

## 3. What \(K/N\) means here

The replication code makes scarcity observable, but it does not produce one self-evident definition of \(K/N\).

### The numerator \(K\)

The code reconstructs each school's available slots from the number of students actually assigned to that school in a cohort. This gives a defensible measure of filled capacity. It is reconstructed from realized assignments rather than read from a separate official capacity table.

### The denominator \(N\)

The raw records contain each student's ranked choices. Several denominators are therefore possible:

1. students ranking the school first;
2. students listing the school anywhere among their choices; or
3. students still competing for the school when it is processed by the centralized assignment algorithm.

These do not mean the same thing. Because students rank several schools and unsuccessful applicants move to later choices, there is no unique school-level \(K/N\) until the scientific meaning of “competing for this opportunity” is specified.

The safest initial measures would be reported separately:

- filled seats divided by first-choice applicants;
- filled seats divided by all students listing the school; and
- the school's assignment cutoff or cutoff percentile.

The cutoff may be the most faithful direct measure of selectivity in this matching system. It should not be silently relabeled as the same \(K/N\) used in a simple one-pool top-\(K\) model.

## 4. Access reality

The public [Zenodo replication package](https://zenodo.org/records/6456606) supplies code, some school-supporting files, expected results, and a detailed README. It does not supply the SEA student records or the principal educational outcome records.

The README states that the SEA applications, scores, choices, and assignments require a research proposal and confidentiality agreement with the Trinidad and Tobago Ministry of Education. It warns that the process may take approximately six months. The Ministry currently maintains a [research application process](https://www.moe.gov.tt/research-application-process-2/) and a [Division of Educational Research and Evaluation](https://www.moe.gov.tt/division-of-educational-research-and-evaluation-dere/).

Several later outcomes require separate records and, in some cases, separate agencies or confidentiality agreements. Consequently, obtaining SEA alone would not automatically reproduce the entire longitudinal study.

The authors report that their full workflow uses Stata, R, and MATLAB and takes about 160 hours after the confidential source data have been acquired. We do not need to reproduce that workflow to use the setting, but it demonstrates that this is not a quick download-and-mosaic candidate.

## 5. Scientific judgment

Trinidad is the strongest candidate on the shortlist because it connects:

\[
\text{prior performance}
\rightarrow
\text{ranked preferences}
\rightarrow
\text{capacity-constrained assignment}
\rightarrow
\text{school/cohort peers}
\rightarrow
\text{later outcomes}.
\]

It also contains a credible source of assignment variation. The published research confirms that school effects are multidimensional: effects on test scores do not fully summarize effects on dropout, arrests, teen motherhood, and formal labor-market participation. See Beuermann, Jackson, Navarro-Sola, and Pardo, [“What is a Good School, and Can Parents Tell?”](https://academic.oup.com/restud/article/90/1/65/6582595).

The important caution is conceptual. This setting provides strong evidence about assignment into secondary schools and subsequent school effects. Scarcity governs entry into the school environment. It does not automatically provide a scarce downstream distinction awarded among classmates after they enter. Those are related but distinct mechanisms.

## 6. Operational decision

1. **Stop the broad external-dataset search.** The Scholar search fulfilled its bounded purpose.
2. **Preserve Trinidad as the best conceptual and identification match.** It belongs in the literature map even if its confidential data are never acquired.
3. **Do not make Trinidad the immediate empirical path.** A stated six-month access process is poorly matched to the current dissertation timetable.
4. **Do not call its selection intensity directly observed without qualification.** Filled capacity is reconstructable; the relevant competitor denominator must be chosen and justified.
5. **Continue the immediate public-data path with NELS:88 and HS&B:80.** They remain available now and identify high-school peer pools, even though they cannot reveal college peer environments without college-destination information.
6. **Reconsider a Trinidad application only as a deliberate parallel or future project.** It should not delay the bounded analysis of data already in hand.

### Later operational update

Item 5 above records the decision before the Romania public archive was identified. Preserve NELS:88 and HS&B:80, but wait for Original VECTOR's Romania schema audit before choosing the immediate empirical dataset. Do not begin a restricted-use application or duplicate the Romania audit while that gate is pending.

## 7. What this check told us, and what it did not

This check told us that a real education setting exists with nearly the complete assignment-sorting-scarcity-outcome structure and credible identification. It also told us that access, rather than scientific fit, prevents it from becoming an immediate replacement dataset.

It did not establish an empirical congestion effect, determine the correct \(K/N\) definition, or show that the same mechanism operates in NELS:88 or HS&B:80. Those questions require data and a separately approved analysis.
