# Companion Volume Lessons Learned

## Purpose
Capture institutional memory from the Menger Companion Volume project.

## What Worked
- Organizing by conceptual units rather than page boundaries
- Exact manuscript anchors
- BLACK BOLD quotations
- BLUE interpretation passages
- RED project connections
- Interwoven commentary rather than isolated sections
- Dense formatting with narrow margins
- Final synthesis chapter
- Cross-cutting appendix

## What Failed
- Invented anchors
- Summary-style chapters
- Excessive compression
- Placeholder chapters
- Sparse quotation density
- Large amounts of whitespace

## Most Valuable Discoveries
### The Underline Test
Every section should contain at least one idea a thoughtful reader would underline.

### The Five-Year Test
Prioritize ideas the reader should still remember years later.

### Mechanism Extraction
Focus on:
1. Phenomenon
2. Mechanism
3. Amplification process
4. Assumptions
5. Boundary conditions
6. Alternative explanations

### Companion Volumes Are Not Summaries
A successful companion volume feels like reading beside an exceptionally insightful co-author.

## Menger-Specific Insight
One of the deepest recurring themes was the transformation:
Difference → Comparison → Ranking → Opportunity → Amplification → Inequality
