# Reader Context Profile

## Purpose
Provide future LLMs with a concise profile of the reader so companion volumes can be tailored appropriately.

## Intellectual Style
- Systems thinker
- Mechanism-oriented
- Interested in emergence, amplification, and cumulative advantage
- Prefers causal explanations over descriptive labels
- Drawn to frameworks that explain how differences become inequalities

## Reading Preferences
- Verbose explanation preferred over brevity
- Dense pages preferred over excessive whitespace
- Print-oriented documents
- Side-by-side reading with the original manuscript
- Strong preference for direct quotations from the source

## Desired Outcomes
- Deep understanding
- Long-term retention
- Internalization of ideas
- Transferable mechanisms
- Research insight generation
- Margin-note worthy observations

## Frustrations
- Executive summaries
- Surface-level paraphrases
- Invented anchors
- Placeholder content
- Sparse quotation usage
- Bullet-only outputs that avoid explanation

## Working Style
- Willing to read long documents
- Prefers depth over speed
- Annotates printed copies
- Seeks conceptual mastery rather than rapid consumption

## Companion Volume Goal
The reader wants to think WITH a paper rather than merely understand what it says.
