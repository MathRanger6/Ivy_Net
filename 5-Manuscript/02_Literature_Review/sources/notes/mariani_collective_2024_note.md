# Mariani et al. 2024: note

**Pages:** Printed page labels are the same as PDF page indices (1–16). In quotes, ligatures are normalized, hyphens at line breaks are rejoined, and citation superscripts are kept as printed (e.g., "seekers6").

## 0. Verified record
Mariani, M. S., Battiston, F., Horvát, E.-Á., Livan, G., Musciotto, F. & Wang, D. "Collective dynamics behind success". *Nature Communications* **15**, 10701 (2024). Review article, 16 pp., 207 refs. doi:10.1038/s41467-024-54612-4.

## 1. Structure
- **p1:** Abstract and introduction.
- **p2:** "The collective nature of success": definition; creation versus reception; scales from individuals to teams to organizations.
- **p3:** Fig. 2 maps four effect types: feedback, temporal, network, identity.
- **p4–6:** "Feedback effects": "Success breeds success" and "Failure breeds success".
- **p6–7:** "Temporal effects": aging of ideas and products; career lifecycles.
- **p7–9:** "Network effects": contagion; "Social ties that influence career success"; "Team structure and success".
- **p9–10:** "Identity effects": gender, homophily, credit.
- **p10–11:** "Outlook": boundary conditions, culture, inequality, interventions, algorithms, merit.

## 2. Definitions
- **Success (p2):** "we broadly define success as the attainment of highly-valued outcomes for an individual, team, or organization – and for the ideas, products, or behaviors which they create or adopt."
- **Collective lens (p1):** "success is not an isolated event; rather, it is the result of the choices and actions by interconnected actors at different scales."
- **Creation (p2):** success is shaped "both in terms of creation (the processes through which an individual, team, or organization acquires resources and generates new ideas, products, or behaviors) and in terms of reception". Cites Barabási 2018, *The Formula*.
- **Success versus performance (p4):** "differences in success do not necessarily reflect differences in performance or quality."
- **Merit (p11):** "the quantification of merit is often elusive."
- **Performance** is never defined. Individual performance is out of scope (p10): "individual psychology and performance … remain outside the scope of this review."

## 7. Helper's suggestion (not from the source)
This review supports three claims: success is collective and not the same as performance; peers, mentors, networks, and prestigious institutions predict or shape career success, sometimes non-monotonically; and selection cutoffs can be arbitrary. It cannot support claims about local competition for fixed slots, frog-pond effects, formal selection rules, or separating sorting from environment effects. Those topics are absent from the review, not disputed by it. We can say this review does not address them, but not that the literature as a whole lacks them.
