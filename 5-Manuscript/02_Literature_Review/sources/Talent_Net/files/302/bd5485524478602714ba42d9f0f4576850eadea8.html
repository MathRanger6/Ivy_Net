The Effects of Mentor Quality, Exposure, and Type on Junior Officer Retention in the United States Army

@inproceedings{Carter2016TheEO,
  title={The Effects of Mentor Quality, Exposure, and Type on Junior Officer Retention in the United States Army},
  author={S. Carter and W. Dudley and David S. Lyle and John Z. Smith},
  year={2016}
}
  • S. Carter, W. Dudley, +1 author John Z. Smith
  • Published 2016
  • Medicine
Despite the prevalence of mentor relationships in the workplace, little is known about their impact on labor market outcomes, including job retention. Using plausibly exogenous assignment of proteges to mentors in the U.S. Army, we find positive retention effects for proteges assigned to high-performing immediate and senior supervisors.
