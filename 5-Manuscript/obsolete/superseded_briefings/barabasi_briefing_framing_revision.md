# Key Revisions to Briefing Framing

## Core theoretical refinement

The project no longer treats:

> “global selection + local opportunity”

as the universal invariant.

Instead, the broader abstraction is now:

\[
\textbf{
finite distinction allocated within correlated local comparison environments
}
\]

This refinement emerged because:
- Army and basketball contain globally constrained advancement,
- but academia contains more localized evaluation and selection structures.

The newer framing therefore generalizes more cleanly across domains.

---

## Revised cross-domain interpretation

| Domain | Comparison Environment | Distinction Constraint |
|---|---|---|
| Army | brigade / SR profile | finite promotions / TBs |
| Basketball | team roster | finite draft slots / playing opportunity |
| Academia | department | finite tenure lines / promotion capacity |

The common invariant is therefore:
- finite distinction capacity,
- inside correlated local environments,
rather than:
- purely global selection systems.

---

## Important consequence

This refinement does NOT invalidate the current Tier 1 modeling work.

Instead:
- “global vs local” becomes a domain-specific structural parameter,
rather than:
- the defining universal mechanism.

The existing modeling framework:
- \(A_i\),
- local quality,
- congestion,
- opportunity,
- finite selection/distinction capacity,
- and correlated assignment

still maps naturally onto all three domains.

---

## Revised candidate language block

> We increasingly suspect that advancement systems may exhibit common dynamics when finite distinction is allocated inside correlated local environments. Strong environments may simultaneously improve development, visibility, and signaling while compressing distinguishability and increasing evaluative congestion.

---

## Revised network science interpretation

This refinement actually strengthens the future Network Science direction because:
- the phenomenon now appears less dependent on one institutional structure,
- and more dependent on how local comparison geometry interacts with constrained distinction capacity.

Potential future directions remain:
- exposure vs comparison networks,
- talent centers of gravity,
- structural distance to concentrated talent,
- and correlated neighborhood effects.
