# Draft Briefing Structure for Laszlo / Alex 

## Working core framing
We investigate whether advancement systems share structural dynamics when individuals compete in correlated local environments under finite global selection. Shorthand: **advancement under constrained distinction.**

## Proposed brief flow (~5 min)

**1. Empirical discovery (~1 min)** — Open with replicated **inverted-U: local pool quality vs advancement probability** in Army officer promotion, NCAA → NBA draft, emerging academia. Interpretation: weak pools disadvantageous; moderate pools beneficial; extremely elite pools may suppress advancement. Posture: empirical regularity first, not causal claims. Figure idea: three small plots (Army / basketball / academia).

**2. Candidate mechanism (~1–1.5 min)** — Hypothesis: advancement is **globally allocated** while recognition and opportunity are **locally structured**. Opposing forces: developmental upside (learning, prestige, exposure, signaling) vs evaluative congestion (comparison compression, reduced distinction, crowding, opportunity competition). Strong environments may raise development/visibility while lowering distinguishability → nonlinear outcomes. Language: **findings solid, mechanisms under construction.**

**3. Why this might generalize (~45 sec)** — Advancement depends on how correlated local environments structure distinction, visibility, congestion, and comparison under finite global selection. Distinction: **global selection** vs **local comparison/opportunity**. Examples: Army local eval → global boards; basketball local minutes/roles → global draft; academia department → prestige markets.

**4. Network science reinterpretation (~1 min)** — Exploratory: local environments as network objects; correlated neighborhoods; exposure vs comparison; talent concentration; distance to elite talent; possible “talent centers of gravity” and geometry of distinction. Anchor sentence (tentative): the inverted-U may reflect how local network geometry structures opportunity, visibility, and distinguishability relative to concentrated talent. Posture: exploratory, not established mechanism.

**5. Minimal generative modeling (~1 min)** — Wang/Yin sequencing: empirical regularity first, minimal mechanism second. Smallest mechanism set that reproduces the nonlinear shape. Tier 1 ingredients: heterogeneous local pools + finite global selection + local opportunity competition → nonlinear advancement. **Empirical replication relatively strong; generative explanation actively under construction.**

## Key strategic positioning
NOT: “we found a quadratic.” IS: replicated cross-domain structural pattern, potentially from correlated local environments under constrained distinction.

## Questions for Laszlo / Alessandro
Does framing fit Science-of-Success? Network models to explore? Is exposure vs comparison theoretically meaningful? Natural geometric/topological formulation? How much generative evidence for a major multidisciplinary venue?

## Tone goal
Confident: empirical replication, local/global distinction, correlated environments. Careful: causality, mechanism certainty, network interpretations.
