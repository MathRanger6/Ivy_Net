# Draft Briefing Structure for Laszlo / Alex

Originally, we were framing the common mechanism as “global selection with local opportunity allocation,” which fit the Army and basketball domains very naturally because promotions and draft slots are globally constrained while evaluations and opportunities are experienced locally. However, once we expanded into academia, we realized that tenure and promotion decisions are much more localized institutionally. That forced us to ask whether “global selection” was truly the invariant mechanism or merely one instantiation of a deeper structure.

We now think the stronger abstraction is that advancement systems allocate **finite distinction capacity inside correlated local comparison environments**. In all three domains, individuals compete within clustered local pools where distinction, visibility, and opportunity are constrained, even if the scale of selection differs. Army promotion boards, NBA drafts, and departmental tenure lines are structurally different systems, but all involve finite evaluative distinction under correlated assignment. That refinement actually strengthens the theory because it generalizes beyond any single institutional architecture while preserving the current Tier 1 modeling framework almost entirely.

## Working core framing

We investigate whether advancement systems share structural dynamics when individuals compete in correlated local environments under finite distinction capacity. Shorthand: **advancement under constrained distinction.**

## Proposed brief flow (~5 min)

### 1. Empirical discovery (~1 min)

Core finding:

\[
\textbf{inverted-U relationship between local pool quality and advancement probability}
\]

Replicated in:

- Army officer promotion
- NCAA basketball → NBA draft
- emerging academic direction

Interpretation:

- weak pools disadvantageous,
- moderate environments beneficial,
- highly elite environments may suppress advancement probability.

Posture:

- empirical regularity first,
- not causal claims.

Figure idea:

- three small empirical plots (Army / basketball / academia).

---

### 2. Candidate mechanism (~1–1.5 min)

Revised working hypothesis:

\[
\textbf{finite distinction is allocated inside correlated local comparison environments}
\]

Potentially opposing forces:

| Developmental upside | Evaluative congestion   |
| -------------------- | ----------------------- |
| learning             | comparison compression  |
| prestige             | reduced distinction     |
| exposure             | crowding                |
| signaling            | opportunity competition |

Interpretation:

- stronger environments may simultaneously increase development/visibility while reducing distinguishability,
- interaction may generate nonlinear advancement outcomes.

Key insight:

- all three domains contain finite distinction capacity,
- but the scope of selection/evaluation differs by domain.

Examples:

- Army: local evaluations + globally constrained promotions
- Basketball: local opportunity allocation + globally constrained draft
- Academia: local evaluation and local tenure capacity inside broader prestige networks

Current posture:

> findings solid, mechanisms under construction.

---

### 3. Why this may generalize (~45 sec)

Current hypothesis:

> Advancement outcomes may depend not only on individual performance, but on how correlated local environments structure distinction, visibility, congestion, and comparison under finite distinction systems.

Important distinction:

| System property        | Examples                           |
| ---------------------- | ---------------------------------- |
| comparison scope       | brigade, team, department          |
| distinction capacity   | TBs, draft slots, tenure lines     |
| correlated assignment  | talent clustering/non-random pools |
| visibility/opportunity | roles, minutes, evaluations        |

Positioning:

- not “we found a quadratic,”
- rather a replicated cross-domain structural pattern.

---

### 4. Network science reinterpretation (~1 min)

Exploratory future direction:

- local environments as network objects,
- correlated neighborhoods,
- exposure vs comparison networks,
- talent concentration,
- structural distance to elite talent.

Potential future ideas:

- “talent centers of gravity”
- hierarchical distance to concentrated talent
- geometry of distinction

Anchor sentence:

> The inverted-U may ultimately reflect how local network geometry structures opportunity, visibility, and distinguishability relative to concentrated talent.

Posture:

- exploratory hypothesis,
- not established mechanism.

---

### 5. Minimal generative modeling (~1 min)

Current philosophy mirrors Wang/Yin sequencing:

- empirical regularity first,
- minimal mechanism second.

Goal:

> identify the smallest mechanism set capable of reproducing the observed nonlinear shape.

Current Tier 1 ingredients:

$\textbf{heterogeneous local pools} + \textbf{finite distinction capacity} + \textbf{local opportunity competition} \rightarrow \textbf{nonlinear advancement outcomes}$

Important refinement:

- “global vs local” is now treated as a domain-specific structural parameter,
- not the universal invariant itself.

Current distinction:

- empirical replication relatively strong,
- generative explanation actively under construction.
