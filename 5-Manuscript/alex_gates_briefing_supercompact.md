## 1. Empirical discovery (~1 min)

**Finding:** replicated **inverted-U relationship** between local pool quality and advancement probability.

Domains:
- Army promotion
- NCAA basketball → NBA draft
- emerging academic direction

Interpretation:
- weak environments disadvantageous,
- moderate environments beneficial,
- highly elite environments potentially suppress advancement probability.

Important posture:

## 3. Why this may generalize (~45 sec)

> Advancement outcomes may depend not only on individual performance, but on how correlated local environments structure distinction, visibility, congestion, and comparison under finite global selection systems.

| Global | Local |
|---|---|
| selection | comparison |
| scarce slots | opportunity allocation |
| promotion/draft | visibility/role/evaluation |

Examples:
- Army: local evaluations → global boards
- Basketball: local roles → global draft
- Academia: local departments → broader prestige markets

**Positioning:** not “we found a quadratic,” but a replicated cross-domain structural pattern.
