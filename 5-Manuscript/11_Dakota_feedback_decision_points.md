## Dakota’s points (plain English)

### 1. Talent compression at the top (generative + theory)

Elite environments **pre-select** talent. Everyone at the top is similarly strong (e.g. talent 98–99 vs 60–80 mid-tier), so **within-pool talent differences are tiny** — hard to rank, advancement may look **near-random** at the most elite tier.

**Manuscript action:** Acknowledge in **§2** (why inverted-U) and **§5** (generative): at elite pools, distinction on “raw talent” may collapse; congestion / non-talent criteria matter more. Optional generative note: uniform or unrankable talent at top pools — **future**, not a v1 rewrite.

### 2. Evaluation is not only “performance”

At the highest levels, promotion uses research **plus** teaching, collegiality, public engagement, etc. — not all observable in your data.

**Manuscript action:** **§4** (tenure) and **Discussion**: state clearly that the model **foregrounds observable research/peer-quality proxies**; other tenure criteria are real but **out of scope for v1**. Not a fatal flaw if labeled.

### 3. Tenure is not “scarce slots” like the NBA (most important)

Basketball has a **fixed number of roster slots**. Tenure is largely **pass/fail**: candidates already won the scarce **assistant-professor line**; tenure committees are **not** choosing among a fixed number of “tenure slots” the way the draft works.

**Manuscript action:** **§4 rewrite priority.** Do not describe tenure as slot-scarcity parallel to NBA. Reframe “constrained distinction” for academia: LOO peer quality / visibility / substitutability within a **binary promotion gate**, not roster competition.

### 4. Clarify what “career outcome” means

Tenure is up-or-out, but **denial ≠ end of career**. At elite places (e.g. Harvard), **tenure rates are very low** — outcome is often **exit to another institution**, not failure.

**Manuscript action:** **§4** define the **estimand** (tenure granted vs not; time-to-event; destination if you have it). Avoid language that equates non-tenure with “elimination from academia.” Label Setting 3 **preliminary** and **institution-specific**.

### 5. Prestige emergence (network direction)

He likes large-scale prestige ideas and points to **Chadrow et al., PNAS** — “Emergence of hierarchy in networked endorsement dynamics.”

**Manuscript action:** **§8** only — add as **exploratory / future work** cite. Do not expand §8 for v1 unless committee elevates.
