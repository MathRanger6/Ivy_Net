- Full empirical dip (hero bin 16 ~1.2% draft rate on real data) is **not** matched in levels — sim uses different K, base rates, and DGP.
- Supports Path II: congestion **in the score** changes advancement curves on poolq_loo; **not** bin-for-bin replication (v1 non-claim).
